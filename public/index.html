About Us

Reasons to choose

Your hair is a reflection of your personality and we know how to bring out the real you. At Serenity Salon, we pride ourselves in customer satisfaction. We ensure that our stylists maintain our team mantra because we know the importance of your hair to your overall look.

We use high quality products that are naturally derived, organic, vegan, cruelty free, paraben/sulfate free, free of harsh chemicals, eco friendly and packaged using recycled products to ensure you and your beauty investment are protected and maintained.
